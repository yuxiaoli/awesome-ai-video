A curated collection of projects for AI-assisted video generation, creative direction, production, and editing.

## AI Video Production & Editing

Agent-driven tools for planning, assembling, and editing footage into finished videos, covering the production work beyond generating individual clips.

- [OpenMontage](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage) - Agentic video production system for researching, scripting, generating assets, sourcing footage, editing, and composing finished videos.
- [video-use](https://github.com/browser-use/video-use) - Video-editing skill for coding agents, using transcripts and visual previews to cut footage, add subtitles, adjust color, and check rendered output.

## AI Video Generation & Direction

Projects that turn ideas, scripts, and stories into generated videos through coordinated writing, storyboarding, visual consistency, and rendering workflows.

- [ViMax](https://github.com/HKUDS/ViMax) - Agentic video creation framework that turns ideas, scripts, or novels into videos through story planning, storyboards, shot generation, and final assembly.
- [Pixelle-Video](https://github.com/ATH-MaaS/Pixelle-Video) - Automated AI short-video engine that creates scripts, generated imagery or clips, voiceovers, background music, and assembled videos from a topic.
- [BigBanana-AI-Director](https://github.com/shuyu-labs/BigBanana-AI-Director) - AI directing platform for short dramas and animated comics, with structured scripts, consistent character assets, keyframe-controlled shots, and video assembly.
